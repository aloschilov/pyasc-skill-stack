Read the boundaries

Simulator results are not NPU results. Fixture coverage is not generation coverage. A completed experiment can contain failed candidates. No overall score combines these different denominators.

Paired effects describe these fixed tasks and runs; the displayed interval does not establish a general skills benefit. Kernel timing ratios and CANNBench service scores are separate metrics.
